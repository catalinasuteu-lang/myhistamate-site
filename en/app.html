The MyHistamate app · on iPhone and Android

Download the app

I already use, day to day, a method that helps me keep my histamine under control — and it genuinely works for me. I've turned it into an app: a journal you fill in in two minutes, foods adjusted to your own tolerances, and an assistant you can ask “why did I feel bad yesterday?”, so you get an answer based on your own journal rather than a general one. It's what I wholeheartedly wish had existed from the start.

The first 7 days are free and it doesn't ask for your card. After that you choose whether to continue, and the subscription can be cancelled any time from the app store.

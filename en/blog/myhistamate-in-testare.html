MyHistamate is in testing — the first people are already using the app

by Cătălina · MyHistamate author · 2 min read

After months of work and testing — and after the method behind the app helped me get through this stretch of my own life more easily — the day I'd been waiting for arrived: MyHistamate is in the hands of its first testers. If you'd like to be one of the people testing it, or simply using it, here's how.

The home screen: your day and your week, at a glance. (The app is bilingual — it follows your language.)

In short: MyHistamate — the journal and guide app for living with histamine intolerance — has officially entered testing. The waitlist stays open: whoever signs up gets a place in the next testing waves, or access from day one at launch.

What MyHistamate does

It's the app I wish I'd had at the start of my own road with histamine intolerance:

  • a daily journal that's actually quick to fill in — meals, symptoms, sleep, stress, in two minutes — shaped around your own case;
  • foods with a histamine score based on the SIGHI list, which you adjust with your own tolerances;
  • an assistant you can write to, which answers based on your journal, not in general terms;
  • scan the menu at a restaurant and find out what's safe for you — plus what to ask the kitchen;
  • guided reintroductions, step by step, so you can widen your list safely.

How the testing works

Invitations go out in the order people join the waitlist, in waves. Every tester gets 30 days of full access, free, no card — and a single "task": use the app normally and tell us honestly what works and what doesn't, straight from the feedback button inside the app.

The account you create during testing stays yours after the official launch — the app simply updates, and your journal isn't lost.

Do you want the app too?

The waitlist is still open. Signing up gets you two things: a shot at a place in the next testing waves and — testing aside — access from day one of the official launch, with the free trial.
